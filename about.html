About Savanna Brew

More than coffee... it's a lifestyle!

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee. Founder Cece Ng'ang'a grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Limuru and wanted to bring that flavour to the city. Today the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month.

Every cup should feel like coming home.

Cece Ng'ang'a, Founder
